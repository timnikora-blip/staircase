Staircase Breakout

The strategy, step by step

  1. Check the market video 1:13exact test is mine
    Chart QQQ and SPY with the 8, 21 and 50 EMAs. Look for shorts only when both close below all three, until the market reclaims them.
  2. Run the short screener video 1:36
    Price over $3, market cap over $300M, EMA8 above price by 0% to 3%, EMA21 above price, 10-day average volume over 500K, ADR at least 3%.
  3. Find the staircase down video 2:02numbers are mine
    A prior leg down of at least 15% or 5× ADR, then a tight base of 5 to 25 days with a range under 3.5× ADR, staying below the averages.
  4. Check the volume video 2:12exact test is mine
    Volume should be high on the sell side. The test requires heavier average volume on down days than up days.
  5. Mark the trigger video 2:25
    The low the stock has respected multiple times.
  6. Short the break video 2:531.5× is mine
    On the 5 minute chart, short the moment it breaks that low with volume. The test uses 1.5× normal pace.
  7. Stop at the high of the day video 3:03
    A move through that high usually means the breakdown failed and a reversal is starting.
  8. Trim into weakness video 3:22sizes and days are mine
    After one or two down days, take some profit without covering it all. The test covers a third on the 2nd down day and a third on the 4th.
  9. Cover the rest on strength video 3:48
    Stay short while it rejects the 8 and 21 EMAs. Cover what's left after a close above both.

Shorting carries risks a long doesn't. A loss has no ceiling, stocks can gap far past a stop, and shares can be recalled or unavailable. Shorting needs a margin account, and borrow fees on small falling stocks can be far above the 3% a year the test charges.
